OlhaAí organizations give a team one place to manage members, recordings, storage, and reporting. Within an organization, spaces and folders keep work organized while roles control who can change settings.

## Start with an organization

An organization has three member roles:

| Role | What it is for |
|------|-----------------|
| Owner | Full control of the organization, including billing and organization settings |
| Admin | Day-to-day administration, member management, and organization-wide workflows |
| Member | Creating, viewing, and collaborating on content they can access |

Owners and admins can invite people from the organization settings. Invitations can be prepared in batches, and OlhaAí also creates missing organization users during a mapped Loom migration without sending an unexpected invitation email.

## Organize work with spaces and folders

Use spaces for durable team areas such as Product, Support, Sales, or Onboarding. Add the organization members who should work in each space; space members can be admins or members. A Pro space can also publish a collection link where anyone with the link can browse the public recordings in that space, and a space can require a password for its recordings.

Folders provide another level of organization inside a space or in a personal library. An OlhaAí can be moved between supported organization, space, and folder locations without changing the recording itself.

A practical structure is:

- One organization for the company or business unit
- Spaces for teams or long-running programs
- Folders for projects, releases, customers, or time periods

## Share and collaborate

Public recordings can be watched by anyone with the link. Private recordings require an authorized signed-in viewer, and password-protected recordings prompt for the password before playback.

Signed-in viewers can add timestamped comments, replies, and emoji reactions when the organization and OlhaAí settings allow them. Comment authors can delete their own comments. New-comment notifications can appear in OlhaAí and by email, depending on notification preferences.

Organization settings can disable comments, reactions, transcripts, captions, summaries, or chapters for the whole organization. They can also set branding, a custom domain, an allowed email domain, a default playback speed, and an AI language.

Owners and admins can also choose to start new recordings Private in organization preferences. The setting is opt-in and leaves existing recordings unchanged. A recording owner can invite an external viewer by email from **Manage access** without adding that viewer as an organization member.

## Understand engagement

Organization analytics can be viewed over 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or the lifetime of the organization. The dashboard includes recording, view, comment, and reaction totals plus time-series and breakdown views for geography, browser, operating system, device, and top recordings. Results can be narrowed to a space or an individual recording.

Analytics are aggregate. They are useful for understanding reach and engagement, but they should not be described as a definitive attendance record for named viewers.

## Use your own storage

An organization can manage storage centrally. OlhaAí currently supports OlhaAí-managed storage, Google Drive, and S3-compatible storage. When storage is managed by the organization, individual members cannot override that choice in OlhaAí Desktop.

Read [Google Drive](/docs/teams/google-drive) for the Drive workflow, or the [S3 guides](/docs/s3-config/aws-s3) for S3-compatible storage.

## Work with an agent

OlhaAí's CLI and MCP surface can inspect organizations, members, spaces, folders, recordings, comments, and analytics. Start by asking the agent to run `cap guide --json` so it uses the command contract installed on your machine.

For example:

> Inspect our OlhaAí organization, members, spaces, and folders. Propose a structure for Product, Support, and Sales, including the exact moves you would make. Do not create, invite, or move anything until I approve the full plan.

The agent should discover IDs rather than infer them from names, remain read-only while planning, and request confirmation before every invitation, role change, content move, storage change, or other mutation. See [Agent Workflows](/docs/agents/workflows) for tested patterns.

## Bringing an existing team over

For a small Loom library, use OlhaAí's importer directly. For a larger organization, map owners and spaces before starting and verify every import reaches a terminal result. The full process is in [Migrating to OlhaAí](/docs/migrating-to-cap).
